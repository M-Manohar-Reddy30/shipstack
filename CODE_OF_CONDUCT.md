# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

SHIPSTACK is a technical open-source project intended for developers of
different backgrounds and experience levels.

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations.
- Welcome constructive technical discussion.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Help maintain a productive engineering environment.

## Contributions

Maintainers may remove comments, discussions, issues, or contributions that
violate this code of conduct.

For serious or repeated violations, maintainers may restrict participation in
the project.
